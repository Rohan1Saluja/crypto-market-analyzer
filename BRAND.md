# Calyrn Brand System

> **Signal, in context.**

Calyrn is a personal crypto intelligence product. It should feel like an intelligence instrument: calm, analytical, precise, evidence-first, and quietly distinctive.

## Positioning

**Category:** Personal crypto intelligence
**Core promise:** Understand what you own, what you are watching, and what deserves your attention.
**Primary tagline:** Signal, in context.
**Product principle:** Markets create noise. Calyrn prioritizes relevance.

## Voice

Use concise, evidence-first language. Prefer "signal", "exposure", "context", "evidence", "risk", "event", and "brief" over hype language.

Avoid casino-style crypto vocabulary, artificial urgency, "100x", "alpha" as a generic marketing claim, and unexplained Web3 jargon.

## Visual system: Mineral Noir

- **Graphite:** near-black foundation; never pure black.
- **Bone:** warm primary text.
- **Mineral jade:** primary brand accent and healthy-state emphasis.
- **Ice:** information, research, and secondary emphasis.
- **Amber:** caution and future-event emphasis.
- **Vermilion:** critical risk only.

The UI should remain low-saturation by default. Risk becomes visually loud only when the information deserves attention.

### CSS tokens

- `--calyrn-jade`
- `--calyrn-ice`
- `--calyrn-amber`
- `--calyrn-vermilion`
- `--calyrn-bone`
- `--calyrn-ink`

Legacy `--spectral-*` variables remain temporary aliases so Phase 2 components inherit the new theme without a risky all-at-once refactor.

## Logo

The mark is three open concentric arcs. They represent:

1. market context,
2. personal exposure,
3. the relevant signal.

The common opening is the point where context becomes actionable information.

Do not add coin symbols, candlesticks, blockchain cubes, gradients inside the mark, or decorative glow around the standalone logo.

## Typography

- **Display:** Space Grotesk
- **UI/body:** Manrope
- **Data:** Geist Mono

Numbers should use tabular figures where possible. Data labels remain uppercase and restrained.

## Core navigation

`Markets · Research · Exposure · Signals`

Markets is the public discovery surface. Exposure and Signals become the personalized product as account infrastructure lands in Phase 3.
